Method · Changelog

What changed, and what was wrong.

Substantive changes to what this site claims, newest first. Not a commit log — an entry appears here when a reader who trusted the previous version would now be misinformed, or when something asserted here turned out to be wrong.

Corrections are rendered at the same weight as additions. A changelog that whispers its errors and announces its features is an advertisement.

Entries

What belongs here

  • A claim the site did not make before — new pages, new figures, a new assertion about how something works.
  • A claim that now says something different — the same subject, a changed conclusion.
  • A correction — the site was wrong. What it said, why it was wrong, and how long it was wrong for.
  • A retraction — a claim withdrawn with nothing put in its place, because the honest answer turned out to be that this site cannot say.

Design changes, refactors, performance work and typo fixes are not here. They are in the repository history, which is the right place for them and the wrong place to look for whether a number moved.
