About

A planning tool, not a prophecy.

An open simulation framework for reasoning about the interconnected systems that determine civilizational outcomes over generational timescales. It exists to make tradeoffs, feedback loops, and the cost of inaction visible — not to predict the future.

What it is — and isn't

Scope

Six interconnected systems — AI, climate, governance, workforce transition, civilization health, and a strategy catalog — each a 0–100 "operating system" with today's score, projections to 2050, and the feedback weights that couple it to the rest. One simulation engine underneath them all.

Honesty

This is scenario analysis, not forecasting. Every claim on this site carries a provenance class, and each is labeled where it appears. What the site computes, and what it does not, is set out on methodology.

The rule they exist to enforce: where the model cannot support a claim, the interface says so rather than filling the gap. Several cards in the action catalog end in an admission that nothing here can score them. That is the honest answer, not a defect. The full specification — what each class means, why they are ordered as they are, and how the ordering is enforced — is on epistemics.

Who made it

One person. Independent, self-funded, no institutional backing, no affiliation, no grant. Built in the open, and wrong in places that have not been found yet.

Corrections are the most useful thing you can send. The changelog records what changed and what turned out to be wrong, with the second given the same prominence as the first.

Code and issues

Everything is on GitHub. Open an issue for a correction, a disputed number, or a claim you think the site cannot support.

What is worth reporting

A number that contradicts its source, a claim with no provenance class, or a place where the interface reads as more confident than the model warrants. All three are defects here.

What this is not

Not a product, not a service, and not advice. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing is collected from you.

Colophon

Stack

Hand-rolled HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework, no build step. three.js powers the 3D instruments. The AI Advisor runs through a thin server proxy or your own key, never a third party.

Type & design

Space Grotesk for display, Inter for text, JetBrains Mono for every number. Feltron-inspired editorial data design; the v3 language adds a real type scale, a validated chart palette, and spring-based motion.

Open

All code, data, and methodology on GitHub. Feedback, corrections, and contributions welcome. Built on the AICIVSIM design system.
