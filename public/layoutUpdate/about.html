About

A planning tool, not a prophecy.

An open simulation framework for reasoning about the interconnected systems that determine civilizational outcomes over generational timescales. It exists to make tradeoffs, feedback loops, and the cost of inaction visible — not to predict the future.

What it is — and isn't

Scope

Six interconnected systems — AI, climate, governance, workforce transition, civilization health, and a strategy catalog — each a 0–100 "operating system" with today's score, projections to 2050, and the feedback weights that couple it to the rest. One simulation engine underneath them all.

Honesty

This is scenario analysis, not forecasting. Every claim on this site belongs to one of three classes — measured, modeled, or authored — and each is labeled where it appears. The distinction is the point; see below.

Measured

Someone counted it.

CO₂, warming, renewable share, emissions and poverty, fetched from NOAA, Our World in Data and the World Bank each time you visit. If a feed fails the page falls back to a baked-in baseline and says so. These are the only numbers on the site that are true independently of us.

Modeled

The engine produced it.

Composite scores, the 0–100 system indices, every projection to 2050, and the five policy levers. These are outputs of one simulation whose assumptions you can read and operate yourself. They are internally consistent and externally unproven — a modeled 28 is not a measured 28.

Authored

A person decided it.

Scope tiers on the action catalog, the dependency edges between actions, the claim that one action corresponds to one model lever, and the narrative prose. Not measurement and not model output — editorial judgement, argued for but not derived. It wears a dashed rule and a mono tag everywhere it appears, and never a scenario colour.

The rule the three classes exist to enforce: where the model cannot support a claim, the interface says so rather than filling the gap. Several cards in the action catalog end in an admission that nothing here can score them. That is the honest answer, not a defect.

Colophon

Stack

Hand-rolled HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework, no build step. three.js powers the 3D instruments. The AI Advisor runs through a thin server proxy or your own key, never a third party.

Type & design

Space Grotesk for display, Inter for text, JetBrains Mono for every number. Feltron-inspired editorial data design; the v3 language adds a real type scale, a validated chart palette, and spring-based motion.

Open

All code, data, and methodology on GitHub. Feedback, corrections, and contributions welcome. Built on the AICIVSIM design system.
